Scalable Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

Developing efficient algorithms for large-scale agent coordination

▼
Problem Statement: Current multi-agent reinforcement learning algorithms struggle to scale beyond a few dozen agents due to exponential growth in state-action space complexity. We need novel approaches that can handle thousands of agents while maintaining learning efficiency and coordination quality.

Key Challenges:
• Exponential state-space growth with agent count
• Communication bandwidth limitations in distributed settings
• Credit assignment in large-scale collaborative tasks
• Real-time decision making with limited computational resources

Expected Impact: Solutions could revolutionize autonomous vehicle fleets, drone swarms, smart grid management, and large-scale logistics optimization.

Explainable Deep Learning for Healthcare

Creating interpretable AI models for critical medical decisions

▼
Problem Statement: While deep learning models achieve high accuracy in medical diagnosis, their black-box nature limits clinical adoption. We need to develop inherently interpretable architectures that maintain high performance while providing clear explanations for medical decisions.

Key Challenges:
• Balancing model complexity with interpretability
• Ensuring explanations are meaningful to medical professionals
• Handling multi-modal medical data (images, text, time-series)
• Meeting regulatory requirements for medical AI systems

Expected Impact: Trusted AI systems for medical diagnosis, drug discovery, and personalized treatment planning that doctors can understand and rely on.

Energy-Efficient Edge AI Computing

Optimizing AI models for resource-constrained environments

▼
Problem Statement: As AI applications move to edge devices, we need models that can deliver high performance while operating under strict power, memory, and computational constraints. Current approaches often sacrifice accuracy for efficiency.

Key Challenges:
• Extreme model compression without significant accuracy loss
• Dynamic inference adaptation based on available resources
• Hardware-aware neural architecture design
• Real-time learning and adaptation on edge devices

Expected Impact: Enabling powerful AI capabilities in IoT devices, autonomous vehicles, wearables, and remote sensing applications with minimal energy consumption.
Join Us

Robust AI Against Adversarial Attacks

Building resilient AI systems for security-critical applications

▼
Problem Statement: Modern AI systems are vulnerable to adversarial attacks that can cause catastrophic failures in safety-critical applications. We need to develop fundamentally robust architectures that maintain performance under adversarial conditions.

Key Challenges:
• Defending against unknown attack strategies
• Maintaining robustness without sacrificing clean accuracy
• Scaling defensive mechanisms to large models
• Theoretical guarantees for robustness bounds

Expected Impact: Secure AI deployment in autonomous systems, financial services, healthcare, and national security applications where reliability is paramount.
