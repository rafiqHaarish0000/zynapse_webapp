PROPORTIONS OF USAGE

Digital products are preferred to be in a light theme with almost equal weight for white and chartreuse. Whereas physical products comes in a dark theme with extensive use of pitch black supported with chartreuse. In both contexts the electric blue is used as accent colour to express the brand language.
